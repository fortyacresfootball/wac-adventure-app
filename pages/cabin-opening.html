Before You Leave

Closing the WAC

Complete the opening steps in reverse order when leaving.

  • Return and secure all recreational equipment.
  • Reattach all chains and locks.
  • Close and lock the garage.
  • Set the heat to 50°F.
  • Turn off the water heater.
  • Turn off the water pump.
  • Turn off the water.
  • During winter, confirm the access-panel light remains on.
  • Secure the cabin and reactivate the alarm.
  • Return the keys to their proper location.
